# Story and dialogue / v0.2

The following narrative text is present in the current English playable. Controls, socket status and objective text are documented in GameScript.md and implemented in FactoryGame.cs. This is a dialogue appendix, separate from the rules-focused Game Script.

## Opening / Pip

Oh! You must be Aunt May's helper. Welcome to the last night shift.

The factory is closing, but there's still one order on my desk. A pumpkin lantern. No readable name... just a child's drawing.

Would you help us finish it? Button left a crate by the striped plate. Try leaving it there to hold the door open.

WASD to walk. Mouse or arrow keys to look. E to pick up or use. Q to place. H for a hint. There's no timer to beat.

## Aunt May's note / optional foyer reading

The helpers only return on Halloween. If the lights are on, please tell them I left the side door open for the parade. Your old drawing is still in the archive. Love, Aunt May.

## Shell completed / Button

A perfect little shell! I kept the mould right where that child left it.

They used to ask who switched off the lights. Not what we'd make them. Who would get home last.

The shell is safe now. Take the power core out and use it on the Ribbon Conveyor.

## The back of the old drawing / required reveal

Under the frame is a note in your own childhood handwriting.

'For the last one to leave. So the way home is bright, too.'

You remember now. The lantern was never waiting for a customer. You drew it for the little helpers who stayed after everyone else went home.

Pip has kept your promise safe all these years. You can finish it tonight.

## Finished lantern / Pip over the speaker

Look at that. Every little crooked line, just like the drawing.

I'll be at dispatch. Shall we see who this one's for?

## Pip / speaking before delivery

One last order, then we can all go home. I'll keep the desk warm.

## Ending

Pip turns your note over once more.

“Then this time, we all clock out together.”

Outside, the parade is just beginning.

## Direction and timing

Reading pauses the world and advances at the player's pace. The note is revealed before the final task, so the player makes and delivers the lantern with its recipient in mind. Completion opens the exit and shows the three helpers walking into the village with the lantern. Optional reading must not be counted as mandatory gameplay length. No voiced performance is currently included.

## Button / optional conversation (v0.4)

Before the shell: “I've kept the old mould polished. Pip said the order might still be wanted someday.” Button explains that the gate's power can be borrowed once a crate holds access.

After the shell: “There it is! A real little shell, just like I remember.” Button directs the player to Ribbon using the same core.

After Ribbon: “I tied the ribbon the way the child liked it. A little crooked. Never trim the ends.” Button points toward the archive and describes Hush as wanting clear aisles.

After the drawing: “We kept your drawing. You came back to finish it. I think that makes this a very good last shift.”

These conversations are optional and use the ordinary paused reading mode.
