# Dialogue appendix for v0.15

Exact English dialogue blocks extracted from FactoryGame.cs. Repeated speaker headings correspond to different progress conditions. Reading is paused and advanceable at the player's pace.

## 1 PIP / ARCHIVE TOUR

Meet Hush, our friendly safety inspector. This archive is safe practice: he will not send you back here.

Power the chime and ring it with E. It calls Hush away. Move the crate to the striped plate while he checks it. You do not need to wait for him to finish.

## 2 PIP / FINAL ASSEMBLY

Your old drawing is safe in the notebook. Let's turn that promise into a real lantern.

The real safety check starts here. If Hush sees you in the active aisle, step behind a shelf or a placed crate. A full warning and contact send you to this room entrance; finished jobs stay safe.

## 3 PIP / NIGHT FOREMAN

Welcome to Willow Works. I'm Pip. Let me show you around, one little step at a time.

We have one forgotten pumpkin lantern to finish tonight. First, let's open this door together. Follow the gold marker; your checklist is on the right.

## 4 TUTORIAL COMPLETE

Your night shift begins. Help Button make the shell, find the old drawing, then bring the finished lantern to Pip.
