# Walking guidance, purpose and crafting feedback / v0.14

Date: October 16, 2026 (America/Chicago). Based on direct user feedback and the supplied blocked-core photograph, not a newly recruited participant study.

## Four reported problems and revisions

1. **Gold route appears to jump left and right while walking.** Previously the planner rebuilt from the moving player on a short timer, while raised route markers faced the current camera and were measured from the new start. A valid route now keeps its world-space corners while the player follows it. Remaining distance and look-ahead advance by projection onto that path. Task changes, substantial deviation or blocked forward segments can still require replanning. Raised markers use the route tangent, not camera orientation. Physics-checked corner geometry is retained; no camera steering or smoothing through walls was added.
2. **Tasks lack an obvious reason.** Each action card now includes a short localized Why sentence. It explains persistent work, the shared core, crate-supported access, the chime, the old drawing and the final gift. H retains the detailed control instruction. Early core retrieval before a crate task offers an existing console as safe storage, instead of telling the player to drop the core on arbitrary floor.
3. **Circuit success and production need clearer feedback.** Connecting all contacts/outlets triggers a bright three-note cue and positive copy. Repeated observation of the same solved state does not replay it. Starting a machine has a separate mechanical cue; the chime has its own bell. Four quiet synthesized work loops follow actual production. The calendar's nearby progress card shows machine, percentage and an actual progress bar. Modal pause stops production and pauses its work loop. Removing a running core explicitly says Stop & retrieve. No voice recording or external audio sample was added.
4. **Core insertion conflicts with a hands-full pickup prompt.** The photograph shows a nearby crate in front of the player while the chime goal is elsewhere. Previously aiming at the crate could produce the second-pickup instruction even though the task said insert the core. Held items no longer cause another pickup to become the focus. Reachable goal sockets have priority; an obstructed or behind-player console gives access/turning guidance and retains the core. E succeeds from the accessible side. The same wall/visibility constraints still apply. Deliberate story reading while carrying a core remains available.
