# First-use revision / v0.6

The user's actual feedback was: intermittently silent launch; small and unattractive text; a crate obscuring the opening wall sign; rules that were hard to understand; no clear next action; and an apparent freeze with no way to quit. They requested clear teaching, arrows and a calendar checklist, and clarified that formal play must also be understandable and fun. English must be the default; additional languages are optional.

A previous informed developer completed v0.5. That did not establish new-player usability. The reported freeze coincides with a dialogue screenshot: dialogue intentionally freezes the world, but old Esc handling and weak continuation cues left this unclear. It is not established that a macOS keyboard conflict caused that incident.

## Changes responding to the observations

- Larger body text and keycaps, Fraunces display headings, Noto Sans body text, shortened control cards and bounded world labels. The foyer sign is above the crate.
- Opening greeting and one-action teaching for look, walk, aim/E, Q placement and door crossing; marker waypoints, valid-placement preview and separate blocked-placement feedback.
- Hanging calendar per chapter, immediate action/reason and core-location notebook throughout Packaging, Archive, Assembly and Delivery.
- Visible shell/bow/tag/light rewards, one-route to branching/contact circuit progression, an optional animated connection example, and safe first Chime practice before Hush detection starts.
- Room introductions that connect story, objective and consequences; gate preparation during engraving; consistent H hints. Completed work survives a safety retry.
- Tab/F1 menu from dialogue and circuits; clear reading-pause text; retained dialogue on resume; visible Quit and menu X shortcut; resizable window.
- Actual restart of stopped music, device-configuration clip rebuilding and an audio test/recovery button.
- Background automatic checks and agent-review launches are silent, even after audio recovery. Normal launches keep sound; headset/system settings are untouched.
- Default English, optional Chinese only after selection. No untested collection of extra translations was added.

## Evidence and limits

Final executed checks and packaging results are recorded in VALIDATION.md. Engine and deterministic traversal checks do not establish enjoyment, independent comprehension, learning or 5–10 minute duration. Agent-operated native observations are identified as such. The intermittent silent first launch was not reproduced; audible sound and mix still require a listener. Existing v0.5 full native completion remains historical evidence, not a v0.6 first-time human test.
