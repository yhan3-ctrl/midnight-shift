# Midnight Shift: The Last Lantern

## High concept
On Halloween night, you return to a closed toy factory and meet the little magical helpers who still look after it. Help finish an old pumpkin-lantern order, discover who it was meant for, and give the ones who stayed late a light for their own walk home.

## Player and experience
A single-player, first-person 3D environmental puzzle game with gentle stealth. Players explore, carry crates, share one power core between a workshop gate and production machines, rotate connected power circuits, and plan deliveries around a predictable safety inspector. The intended audience enjoys warm characters and readable spatial puzzles, without requiring horror or combat experience.

## Core promise
Finishing a practical job becomes a personal act of care. The player's childhood drawing reveals that the last order was a gift for the helpers themselves. The player learns this before assembling and physically delivering the lantern.

## Learning target
Plan and revise a sequence of actions under shared-resource constraints. Distinguish completed outputs that persist without power from access that depends on a continuously held condition. Read reciprocal circuit connections, route through required contacts and split one supply between outlets. These are the abilities directly exercised by the required progression.

Patrol prediction, placed-crate occlusion and machine-sound distraction are supported strategies. A fast player may escape pursuit without deliberately using all of them, so successful completion alone does not demonstrate mastery of those strategies.

These are intended learning outcomes. Their necessity and effectiveness must be evaluated against real player behaviour.

## World, progression and audio
Four authored circuit boards progress from one route to branching supply and required intermediate contacts. Four connected factory areas move from safe crate teaching to shared power, archive traversal and final assembly. A repeating original music-box melody gains plucked accompaniment as work resumes. Warm teal, brass, autumn orange and gentle ghosts establish Halloween without jump scares.

## Scope and status
Unity 6.6, 3D, keyboard/mouse. Intended platforms: Windows, macOS, Linux. Target first-run duration: 7–9 minutes, not yet measured. Version 0.6 adds a stepwise guide, hanging-calendar goals throughout the formal game, safe initial Chime practice, and parallel gate preparation during engraving. Version 0.7 keeps English as default and offers Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Japanese and Korean for UI, guidance and story. Version 0.5 completed a developer-operated native playthrough with the inspector active, including one safety retry and the animated ending. Independent first-time human tests, audio listening and cross-platform execution remain pending. See GameScript.md for implemented rules and current gaps.
