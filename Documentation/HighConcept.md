# Midnight Shift: The Last Lantern

## High concept
On Halloween night, you return to a closed toy factory and meet the little magical helpers who still look after it. Help finish an old pumpkin-lantern order, discover who it was meant for, and give the ones who stayed late a light for their own walk home.

## Player and experience
A single-player, first-person 3D environmental puzzle game with gentle stealth. Players explore, carry crates, share one power core between machines, and plan deliveries around a predictable safety inspector. The intended audience enjoys warm characters and readable spatial puzzles, without requiring horror or combat experience.

## Core promise
Finishing a practical job becomes a personal act of care. The player's childhood drawing reveals that the last order was a gift for the helpers themselves. The player learns this before assembling and physically delivering the lantern.

## Learning target
Plan and revise a sequence of actions under shared-resource constraints. Observe which outputs persist without power and which routes depend on a continuously held condition. Predict a patrol and use environmental occlusion or a machine's sound to create an opportunity to cross.

These are intended learning outcomes. Their necessity and effectiveness must be evaluated against real player behaviour.

## World, progression and audio
Four connected factory areas move from safe crate teaching to shared power, archive traversal and final assembly. A repeating original music-box melody gains plucked accompaniment as work resumes. Warm teal, brass, autumn orange and gentle ghosts establish Halloween without jump scares.

## Scope and status
Unity 6.6, 3D, keyboard/mouse. Intended platforms: Windows, macOS, Linux. Target first-run duration: 7–9 minutes, not yet measured. Version 0.1 is an early playable, not a finished or playtested submission. See GameScript.md for implemented rules and current gaps.
