# Playtesting record

No human playtests have been recorded for this version. Do not invent names, durations or feedback.

For each participant record:
- Build/version and test date
- Experience with first-person games and whether the participant has seen the solution
- Completion / abandonment, active time, reading time, retries and external help
- First unclear instruction or mistaken rule inference
- Actual power sequence and crate arrangement
- Whether machine sound is used intentionally
- Their own explanation of who the lantern is for
- Concrete observation or quote, resulting change and retest

Engine integration checks are recorded separately in VALIDATION.md. They are not human playtests.
